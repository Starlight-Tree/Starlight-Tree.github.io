I build web apps with Go, FastAPI, Next.js and Postgres: screens, API, database and tests, with design decisions kept in written records (ADRs).

Projects

ShopOrder, DocuNavi and JobNavi are demos set in a fictional world (the planet Zahard) and use no real shops, people or documents. TownScan uses real aerial photos.

ShopOrder

Go · Next.js · TypeScript · Postgres · Docker Compose

An ordering app for a fictional café chain: pickup and delivery for customers, a prep board and stock for staff, analytics for managers. Stock is an append-only ledger, so the same item is never sold twice.

DocuNavi

FastAPI · LangGraph · Gemini · pgvector · Next.js

A RAG chatbot that answers from PDF documents and shows its sources. The sample documents are fictional.

JobNavi

Go · Next.js · Postgres

A job board for botanical gardens on a fictional planet: search and detail pages for job postings.

TownScan

Go · Next.js · Postgres · Gemini · Python (building extraction)

A chat app that finds candidates for new buildings in the south-western suburbs of Paris from 2014 and 2024 aerial photos, and answers with a short text and one view: photos, a chart or a map. The app's screens are in Japanese. A local prototype; accuracy is not measured yet.

The photos shown are the four largest "not detected in 2014" candidates and are not necessarily typical of the detection quality. Map and photos: Plan IGN (last updated 2026-10-08) and BD ORTHO (last updated 2026-11-18) by IGN, Licence Ouverte 2.0.
